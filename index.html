About me

Irina Serebriannikova is a painter and art teacher. She graduated from the Faculty of Painting of the Surikov Moscow State Academic Art Institute. She paints landscapes, portraits and still lifes. She has taken part in many exhibitions in Moscow and in exhibition projects in Montenegro. Her works are in private collections in Russia, the USA and Spain.

Since 2022 Irina has headed the art department of the «Path to Success» network of private schools in Moscow. She is the author of the courses ART WAY, «Art Immersion» and «Art Design», and curates student exhibitions and theatre productions. She also came up with the format of family art trips.

Since 2021 Irina has run her own project: creative art breakfasts for adults and companies. She is currently studying at the Faculty of Art History of the Agena Art International Academy of Art and Antiques.
